Speech-style capture

Not a generic voice.
Their way of speaking.

A voice clone alone is not enough. During setup, the person reads a short, comfortable paragraph, or the family shares clear existing recordings. We listen for the structure beneath the sound: their usual phrases, sentence length, pauses, humor, nicknames and how they answer a question.

THE SIMPLE SETUP1 minute reading + 1 home video

Voice floor: 1 minute of clean speech. Better: 3–5 minutes. Speech structure: a 60–90 second guided passage plus a couple minutes of natural talking in a home video or story.
